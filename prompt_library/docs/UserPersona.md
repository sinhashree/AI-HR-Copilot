# Primary User Persona

## Sarah Johnson

**Role:** Technical Recruiter

**Industry:** Information Technology

**Experience:** 6 Years

**Age:** 30

**Location:** Bengaluru, India

---

# About Sarah

Sarah is a Technical Recruiter working for a fast-growing technology company. She manages the complete recruitment lifecycle, including sourcing candidates, coordinating interviews, creating job descriptions, and collaborating with hiring managers.

She handles multiple hiring requests simultaneously and often needs to publish new job openings within tight deadlines.

---

# Goals

- Create professional job descriptions quickly.
- Reduce repetitive manual work.
- Maintain ATS-friendly formatting.
- Ensure consistency across job postings.
- Improve hiring efficiency.

---

# Pain Points

- Writing job descriptions from scratch is time-consuming.
- Frequent revisions requested by hiring managers.
- Inconsistent formatting across departments.
- Difficulty remembering all required job details.
- Tight hiring deadlines reduce productivity.

---

# Motivations

Sarah wants tools that help her:

- Save time.
- Improve productivity.
- Standardize documentation.
- Focus more on candidate engagement rather than repetitive writing.
- Leverage AI to automate routine tasks.

---

# Daily Workflow

1. Receive hiring request.
2. Meet with hiring manager.
3. Collect role requirements.
4. Draft job description.
5. Review with stakeholders.
6. Publish job opening.
7. Begin candidate sourcing.

---

# Frustrations

- Rewriting similar job descriptions repeatedly.
- Missing important role requirements.
- Switching between multiple documents.
- Manual formatting.
- Last-minute hiring requests.

# User Needs

Sarah needs a solution that:

- Is simple to use.
- Requires minimal training.
- Produces structured prompts.
- Allows quick export of generated content.
- Can integrate with AI tools.

---

# User Story

**As a Technical Recruiter,**

I want to generate structured job description prompts quickly,

So that I can reduce manual effort, maintain consistency, and publish hiring requirements faster.

---

# Success Criteria

Sarah considers the product successful if she can:

- Generate a job description prompt in less than 3 minutes.
- Export it in multiple formats.
- Reuse prompts for similar positions.
- Spend less time on documentation.

---

# How AI HR Copilot Solves Her Problems

| Problem | Solution |
|----------|----------|
| Manual documentation | Guided prompt generation |
| Repetitive work | Reusable prompt templates |
| Formatting issues | Standardized structure |
| Time constraints | Quick prompt creation |
| Multiple export needs | TXT, Word, and PDF downloads |

---

# Future Expectations

Sarah would like future versions to include:

- AI-generated job descriptions.
- Resume screening.
- ATS score calculation.
- Interview question generation.
- HR email drafting.
- Saved prompt library.
- Team collaboration features.

---

# Product Design Decisions Influenced by This Persona

Based on Sarah's needs, the product prioritizes:

- Simple and intuitive interface.
- Minimal required inputs.
- Fast prompt generation.
- Easy document export.
- Future AI integration without increasing complexity.

---

# Persona Summary

Sarah represents busy recruiters who need an efficient, user-friendly solution for creating professional hiring documentation. AI HR Copilot is designed to reduce repetitive work, improve consistency, and streamline recruitment workflows while providing a foundation for future AI-powered HR automation.
